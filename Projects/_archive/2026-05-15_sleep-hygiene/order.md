# Brief

## 1. Purpose
Persuade workaholic founders that sleep hygiene is not a soft self-care
indulgence but a competitive lever. The deck should leave them ready to
adopt one or two concrete habits this week.

## 2. Single takeaway
Sleep is the highest-leverage performance investment a founder can make,
and it compounds.

## 3. Anything the agent should know
- Audience is allergic to wellness-industrial-complex tone. Lead with
  performance, output, and decision quality — not vibes.
- Short deck (≤ 6 slides). Every beat must earn its place.
- One-pager artifact will be circulated to co-founders / chiefs of staff
  who didn't see the live talk; it should stand alone.
