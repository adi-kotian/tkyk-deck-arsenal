# Production order — Taikyoku Seed Information Pack

**What:** a seed-stage information pack (fuller than a pitch — the doc
an investor reads alone) for **Taikyoku**, an agentic company building
AI agents for **owner-level assurance of capital projects and capital
assets**.

**The company (semi-fictional):** Owners of large capital projects
(energy, infrastructure, data centers, industrial plants) and the
assets those projects become are structurally blind between milestone
reports. Assurance — is it on cost, on schedule, build-quality
conforming, commissioning-ready, performing to design — is manual,
sampled, and late, and it largely relies on the people being assured.
Taikyoku deploys always-on AI agents that give the **owner** (not the
EPC/contractor) independent, continuous assurance.

**Why now:** capital project delivery is a multi-trillion-dollar
annual spend with a notorious overrun problem; agentic AI can finally
ingest project-controls data, contractor reporting, documents and
asset telemetry continuously rather than at audit points.

**Deliverable:** one Taikyoku deck rendered via open-slide, composed
from the Tier 1 library. Mono-English. ~14–16 pages. One assigned gold
moment (the ask). No breath. Title Case headlines.

**Out of scope:** real financials, real customer names, real team
identities. All invented, seed-plausible, flagged semi-fictional.
