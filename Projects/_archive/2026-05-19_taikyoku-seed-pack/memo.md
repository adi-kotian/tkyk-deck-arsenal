# Memo — Taikyoku Seed Information Pack

## Pitch (one line)

Taikyoku builds AI agents that give the **owners** of capital projects
and assets independent, continuous assurance — replacing assurance
that is manual, sampled, late, and authored by the party being assured.

## Thesis

Capital project delivery is a multi-trillion-dollar annual spend with a
structural assurance gap: the owner learns the truth about cost,
schedule, quality and performance from monthly reports written by the
contractor delivering the work. Agentic AI closes that gap — always-on
agents that independently watch the project and the asset on the
owner's behalf. The wedge is assurance; the compounding asset is a
cross-project, cross-owner **assurance graph** no incumbent can build
because incumbents sell to the contractor, not the owner.

## Key points (the spine of the deck)

1. **The problem is owner blindness, not lack of data.** Industry
   studies put ~9 in 10 megaprojects over budget, ~80% average cost
   overrun, frequent multi-quarter schedule slips. Owners see this
   through milestone reports authored by the EPC/contractor — the party
   being assured. Independent assurance today is manual, sampled, and
   arrives after the money is spent.
2. **What Taikyoku does.** Owner-side AI agents run continuous
   assurance across four lanes: **cost & schedule integrity**,
   **change-order & claims scrutiny**, **quality & commissioning
   conformance**, **asset performance vs design** (post-handover).
   They ingest project-controls data, contractor reporting, RFIs and
   change orders, documents, and asset telemetry.
3. **How it works.** Connect → the agents establish an independent
   baseline → continuously reconcile reported vs. inferred status →
   surface exposures and substantiated findings to the owner with
   evidence → feed outcomes back into the assurance graph.
4. **Why it's defensible.** Owner-side trust (we are not the
   contractor's tool), and an assurance graph that compounds across
   every project and asset assured — patterns of overrun, claim, and
   underperformance that get sharper with scale.
5. **Traction (invented, pilot-stage).** 4 design-partner owners;
   **$6.2B** aggregate project value under assurance in pilots;
   **$184M** of cost/schedule exposure and 2 material claims surfaced
   in the first 90 days; pilot ROI ≈ **11×** on fees; 2 pilots
   converting to paid.
6. **Market.** Serviceable ≈ **$12B** today (project controls +
   owner's-engineer / assurance software and services), expanding as
   agentic AI shifts assurance spend from services to software against
   a multi-trillion annual capital-project base.
7. **The ask.** Raising **$7M seed**, ~18-month runway: convert the 2
   paid pilots, scale to ~8 owners, build the agent platform + the
   assurance graph, and hire agent engineering, owner's-engineer domain
   leads, and GTM.

## Positioning

Against three alternatives, Taikyoku is the only **owner-side,
continuous, independent** option: (a) EPC/contractor self-reporting —
not independent; (b) periodic owner's-engineer audits — independent but
manual and sampled; (c) project-controls software — data, not
assurance, and sold to the deliverer. Taikyoku is independent **and**
continuous **and** owner-aligned.
